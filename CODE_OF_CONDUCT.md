# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository is my main GitHub profile repository. It represents my public technical presence: projects, writing, and the kind of collaboration I'm open to.

The goal is to keep discussions friendly, useful, and grounded — for anyone interested in AI engineering, full-stack development, or the projects listed here.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Talk about the ideas, code, and tradeoffs, not the person.
- Give feedback that's specific and easy to act on.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something's unclear.
- Respect boundaries around private work, unpublished ideas, and personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not okay:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Spam, unrelated self-promotion, or low-effort solicitation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing exploit details, secrets, or tokens in public issues or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to interactions in this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and any collaboration that starts here.

## Enforcement

I may edit, hide, delete, or lock content that violates this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may lead to blocking or reporting through GitHub.

If something needs attention, reach out through the channels listed in the README.
